# Third-party assets

## Fonts

Playfair Display and Plus Jakarta Sans are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their upstream license notices are included in `assets/licenses/` and are copied into the deployed site with the fonts.

- Playfair Display: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/playfairdisplay
- Plus Jakarta Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/plusjakartasans

## Design and images

The original design export and illustrative images were supplied for this project through Google Stitch. The original export is preserved in `stitch_boutique_salon_workspace_marketplace/`. Local prototype assets were downloaded from the image sources referenced in that export.

All salon listings, names, conversations, prices and availability in the prototype are fictional demonstration content.
