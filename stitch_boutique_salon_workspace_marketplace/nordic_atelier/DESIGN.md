## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a warm, Scandinavian boutique sensibility tailored for independent beauty and wellness professionals—hairstylists, skin therapists, lash and brow artists, massage therapists, and holistic practitioners seeking rental chairs, private treatment cabins, and studio workspaces.

The visual direction rejects cold, transactional enterprise tech in favor of elevated hospitality:
- **Atmosphere**: Intimate, sunlit, clean, and sensory. It mirrors the feeling of stepping into an exclusive, high-end day spa or minimalist Scandinavian studio.
- **Tone**: Calm authority paired with supportive feminine warmth. It positions renting a salon chair or treatment suite not as a friction-heavy commercial lease, but as an aspirational career milestone.
- **Aesthetic Direction**: High-end editorial meets Scandinavian soft-minimalism. Generous negative space, delicate warm hair-line dividers, tactile rounded envelopes, and refined typographic contrast between high-fashion editorial serifs and crisp, utilitarian geometric sans-serifs.
- **Perceived Experience**: Effortless, secure, curated, and respectful of the artisan's craft and client-facing reputation.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in tactile organic materials, warm stone, and refined botanicals. It completely avoids synthetic tech blues or harsh pure blacks.

### Roles and Application
- **Canvas (`#FCF8F5`)**: A soft, luminous warm cream that serves as the universal backdrop. It maintains a gentle reading contrast without blinding glare.
- **Primary Brand / Action (`#813B5C`)**: A deep velvet plum-rose used for key calls-to-action, active selection outlines, key stat highlights, and critical links. It provides dependable AA/AAA contrast over light surfaces while communicating boutique luxury.
- **Secondary / Container Tint (`#F4E4E9`)**: A soft powder blush utilized for nested surfaces, cards, filtering chips, and badge backgrounds.
- **Decorative Accent (`#E8B8A6`)**: Muted peach/terracotta applied intentionally for subtle micro-interactions, availability indicators, rating highlights, and delicate atmospheric gradients.
- **Deep Plum Text Neutral (`#33252E`)**: A dark plum-tinted espresso replacement for standard black. It renders typography rich, soft, and readable without harsh graphical severity.
- **Subtle Surface Stroke (`#EBDCD7`)**: A warm rose-chalk outline token used to shape cards, inputs, and dividers without visually fragmenting layouts.

## Typography

The type system balances evocative editorial storytelling with high-density marketplace legibility:
- **Playfair Display**: Used exclusively for primary page statements, boutique location titles, showcase price callouts, and section titles. Its high contrast and delicate serifs evoke high-fashion editorials and upscale beauty magazines.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Handles the structural UI responsibilities—listing metadata (m², chair amenities, wash stations), filter parameters, calendar slots, form inputs, button CTA labels, and operational data. Its geometric cleanliness prevents visual fatigue.

### Typographic Rules
- Restrain Playfair Display to short headlines; avoid multi-sentence blocks.
- Set price numerals in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold to ensure unambiguous, high-clarity transactional comprehension across compact phone viewports.
- Keep body tracking neutral to slightly open (`0` to `0.01em`) to maintain airy lightness.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional rhythm of breathing room and composed clusters, mimicking curated studio interior floor plans.

### Grid & Page Structure
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` gutters. Horizontal scrolling rails (carousels) are utilized for venue amenity chips and photo previews, bleeding `1rem` to the screen edge.
- **Tablet (768px–1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins. Content groups (venue details and reservation modules) sit side-by-side.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1320px`, centered, with `3.5rem` margins and `2rem` gutters. Listings use a split-pane rhythm: immersive gallery and details on the left (7 columns), sticky reservation request card on the right (5 columns).

### Spacing Philosophy
- Group related functional items (e.g., salon address + chair rating + price badge) tightly using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Allow structural blocks (e.g., photo hero to host profile; host profile to rental terms) to breathe with `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is organic, soft, and warm—mimicking natural daylight filtering through studio curtains rather than artificial grey drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Palette
- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Pill Badges)**: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(129, 59, 92, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(51, 37, 46, 0.04)`. Imparts subtle weight without hard edges.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls)**: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(129, 59, 92, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(51, 37, 46, 0.04)`. Elevates the card forward gently.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Bar, Modals, Drawer Sheets)**: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(81, 37, 58, 0.14), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(51, 37, 46, 0.06)`.

### Layering Rules
- **Tonal Grouping**: Prefer placing cards (`#F4E4E9` or `#FFFFFF`) against the `#FCF8F5` background, paired with a `1px` stroke of `#EBDCD7`, instead of relying on heavy shadows alone.
- **Backdrop Frosting**: Overlays and mobile bottom-sheet backdrops use `rgba(252, 248, 245, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` to maintain luminous ambiance.

## Shapes

The shape system relies on fluid, generous radii (pill and hyper-rounded forms) that reflect natural curves, mirrors, ceramic basins, and comfortable salon furnishings.

### Radius Distribution
- **Cards, Treatment Modules, Modals**: `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem to 2rem). Corners never feel sharp or defensive.
- **Buttons, Filters, Search Bars, Badges**: Full pill (`9999px` / `rounded-full`). These invite touch interactions on mobile devices.
- **Images and Gallery Previews**: Scaled with `rounded-2xl` to harmonize with container outlines. Inner thumbnail elements use `rounded-xl`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Chair / Contact Salon)**: Background `#813B5C`, text `#FCF8F5`, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `48px` (mobile `52px` for thumb ergonomics). Padding: `0 24px`. Hover: transitions to deep plum-black `#6A2E4B` with Level 2 warm shadow.
- **Secondary (Explore Amenities / Filter)**: Background `#F4E4E9`, text `#813B5C`, border `1px solid #EBDCD7`. Hover: `#EBDCD7` background fill.
- **Ghost / Minimalist**: Transparent background, text `#813B5C`, hover background `rgba(244, 228, 233, 0.5)`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Base State**: Height `36px`, pill shape, background `#FCF8F5`, border `1px solid #EBDCD7`, text `#33252E`, typography `label-md`.
- **Selected State**: Background `#813B5C`, border `1px solid #813B5C`, text `#FCF8F5`.
- **Category Icons**: Micro salon icons (scissors, skincare drop, nail polish, massage stones) tinted to match current text color.

### Marketplace Cards (Listing & Chair Cards)
- **Container**: White (`#FFFFFF`) or `#F4E4E9` base, border `1px solid #EBDCD7`, radius `1.5rem`, Level 1 shadow.
- **Media Header**: Aspect ratio `4:3` (mobile) or `16:10` (desktop), `rounded-xl` internal clips, top-right wishlist pill button (`#FCF8F5` translucent fill with plum heart icon).
- **Meta Block**: Location label in `body-sm` (`#813B5C`), title in `headline-sm` (`#33252E`), rental frequency tag ("Per dag" / "Per måned") in `label-sm` with decorative accent highlight (`#E8B8A6`).

### Form Inputs & Date/Time Selectors
- **Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EBDCD7`, radius `1rem`, height `48px`, font `body-md` in `#33252E`.
- **Focus State**: Border color `#813B5C`, subtle box-glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(129, 59, 92, 0.12)`.
- **Calendar & Time Pickers**: Selected date slots use circular `#813B5C` fills; partially booked/waitlist dates display an `#E8B8A6` lower dot indicator.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Style**: Custom circular radio and `rounded-md` checkbox.
- **Active State**: Fill `#813B5C` with clean white icon check mark; non-active uses border `1.5px solid #EBDCD7` over `#FFFFFF`.

### Dedicated Industry Components
- **Host / Salon Owner Profile Pill**: Floating circular avatar (`44px`) accompanied by verification check and salon studio name, establishing mutual trust between chair renters and hosts.
- **Equipment & Amenity Matrix**: Compact icon grid (Backwash Unit, Autoclave, Reception Support, Hydraulic Chair, Natural Daylighting) framed within `rounded-xl` light blush tiles (`#F4E4E9`).
- **Sticky Mobile Booking Dock**: Fixed bottom floating bar with frosted glass blur (`rgba(252, 248, 245, 0.9)`), showing total rental rate alongside a primary pill CTA.
